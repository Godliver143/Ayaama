📱 iPhone: add it to Calendar
  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone and tap Download my calendar. Or AirDrop, text, or email the file to the phone and tap it.
  2. Tap Add All.
  3. Choose a calendar (or make a new one called “School”), then tap Done.
  4. To get alerts, allow notifications for Calendar in Settings → Notifications → Calendar.
🤖 Android and Google Calendar
  1. Download the file. On a phone it goes to Downloads. Importing is easiest from a computer.
  2. Open Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export. Tip: make a new calendar first (Settings → Add calendar → Create new calendar) so you can remove everything at once later.
  3. Choose the .ics file, pick the calendar, and click Import. It then shows up in the Google Calendar app on the phone.
  4. Google Calendar doesn't keep alerts from imported files. To get alerts, open that calendar's settings and set default notifications (for example, 15 minutes before).
Need to change something later?

Your choices are saved on this device only. Come back, make changes, and download again. Before importing the new file, delete the old events (or the whole “School” calendar) so nothing shows up twice.
